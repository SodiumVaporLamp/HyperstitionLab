# Hyperstition Lab

An interactive page for playing with AQ gematria on the Ccru's Numogram (the Decimal Labyrinth).

**LIVE:(https://sodiumvaporlamp.github.io/HyperstitionLab/)

## What is this?

**Gematria** is the old practice of giving each letter a number, so that every word or phrase adds up to a value. Words with the same value are read as secretly linked. It comes from Hebrew tradition, and English versions are now common online (Ordinal, where A = 1 and Z = 26, is the simplest). **AQ**, or Alphanumeric Qabbala, is the English cipher used by the **Ccru** (Cybernetic Culture Research Unit), an experimental group that came together around the University of Warwick in the late 1990s, including Nick Land, Sadie Plant and Mark Fisher. Its work mixed philosophy, cybernetics, occultism and science fiction, and it coined "hyperstition": fictions that make themselves real. AQ simply keeps counting past 9 the way base-36 notation does, so A = 10, B = 11, and so on up to Z = 35.

The **Numogram** is the Ccru's map of the numbers 0 to 9, credited in their texts to a Professor D. C. Barker, whom many readers take to be one of the Ccru's own fictions. It is built entirely from simple digit arithmetic. The ten digits are its zones, and they pair up into five "syzygies" that each sum to nine. Each pair's difference sends a "current" to another zone, which carves the map into a central Time-Circuit and two outer regions, the Warp and the Plex. Each pair of zones also has a demon, giving 45 in all. This page places a phrase on the map by adding up its AQ value, then adding the digits until one is left; that digit is its zone. The page does the arithmetic and draws the results.

What was it *for*? Ccru texts present the Numogram as recovered rather than invented, and treat it as a working instrument rather than a theory: a map of time with a looping Time-Circuit and an Outside, and a register of demons. Part of the appeal is the appeal of divination. Fixed rules hand you readings you didn't choose, and the meaning comes from what you make of them. The Ccru's word for fictions that make themselves real through use was hyperstition, and the Numogram was one of its main instruments.

**Inherited and added.** The diagram, its zones, currents, gates and demons, and the AQ cipher come from Ccru texts. Reading AQ totals through the diagram has a published precedent in Nick Land's "Qabbala 101" (*Collapse* I, 2007). A footnote totals UNITY as 30 + 23 + 18 + 29 + 34 = 134 and reduces it to 8, which the text calls the Numogrammatic twin of 1, numerology's number for unity. The same passage reduces OPPOSITION (238 → 13 → 4), COMPLETION (212 → 5) and CONJUNCTION (237 → 12 → 3). Whether the wider Ccru worked this way is a separate question. The phrase pairs, searches and experiments built on top are this project's own.

## How this project started

I was reminded of an old [@xenocosmography](https://x.com/xenocosmography) post about Sphinx phrases (four letters, two letters, three letters, like *What is Man?* and *Will to Ice*), their gematria sums, and patterns that arise from comparing sums. That turned into a morning of searching, both between and across different gematria cyphers, and finally mapping everything onto the Numogram. These pages give tools so users can explore more themselves.

## The five tabs

| Tab | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Zones** | Type two phrases. Each one lands in a zone, and the page draws the demon that stands between the two zones, along with their currents, gates, and the zone each of six ciphers gives. For fuller demon readings, see the Divination mode in [Cyphers](https://cyphers.news). |
| **Alphabet** | The 26 letters on a ring, each joined to its mirror (A/Z, B/Y … M/N). Every mirror pair sums to 45 in AQ. Includes a word mirror and a table of gate names. |
| **Demons** | One experiment on the Pandemonium Matrix's 45 demons: totalled in AQ, does each name land in one of its own two zones? It estimates how surprising the result is and explains the method. For the demons' titles, aliases and classes, see the Pandemonium catalogue in [Cyphers](https://cyphers.news). |
| **Loops** | Cipher-hop loops, where one phrase's value in one cipher equals the next phrase's value in another, all the way around. Five curated loops are animated, and a finder searches about 650 built-in phrases for loops through anything you type. The full phrase list and the ranking formula are shown on the page. |
| **Attractor** | Spell a number out, add up its letters, spell out the total, and repeat. Every start falls into a loop. Pick Ordinal, AQ or Satanic and see where each loop sits on the Numogram. |

Each tab has its own link: add `#zones`, `#alphabet`, `#demons`, `#loops` or `#attractor` to the page URL.

## Companion: Time-Sorcery

**LIVE: (https://sodiumvaporlamp.github.io/HyperstitionLab/time-sorcery.html) is a nine-step walkthrough that turns the same practices on the future. Give it a phrase and a year. It counts the phrase the way Land's "Primitive Numerization" does, totals and plexes it, and dates the year in the Ccru's K-Time, a year count that starts from 00 = AD 1900, the same scheme as the two-digit dates behind Y2K. Then it names the demon between the two zones, walks that demon's published rites backwards from the year to the phrase, writes both numbers in Barker's Tic Xenotation, and files the result as a transmission you can copy. Each step marks which part comes from the Ccru's texts or "Qabbala 101" and which part is the page's own.

One finding from building it: all 75 rite paths published in the Pandemonium Matrix can be walked with three moves, crossing a syzygy, riding its current, or taking a gate. The rite paths were transcribed from the Ccru's Pandemonium page and match the independent transcription in [Cyphers' source](https://github.com/Cyphers-News/gematro-hyperdope) for all 45 demons.

## Companion: Vanishing Point
**LIVE: https://sodiumvaporlamp.github.io/HyperstitionLab/vanishing-point.html ) puts 162 milestones in the history of AI, and of the numbers, ledgers and machines behind it, on a zoomable logarithmic spiral. Each turn holds a tenth of the time of the turn outside it: millennia on the rim, then centuries, decades, years and months toward the centre. On a logarithmic spiral, zooming in and turning are the same motion, so travelling through it feels like a tunnel.

The centre is the date in the title of Heinz von Foerster, Patricia Mora and Lawrence Amiot's 1960 *Science* paper, "Doomsday: Friday, 13 November, A.D. 2026", which fitted world population to a curve that reaches infinity in finite time. You can move the centre to now, or to Ray Kurzweil's 2045.

Five lanes run side by side: Capital, Reckoning, Learning, Language and Hyperstition. Hand-written rules and other attempts to type knowledge in, the approaches that lost to learning from data, are drawn hollow; a Bitter Lesson switch hides them. Every card separates what Land or the Ccru actually wrote ("From the texts", quoted and cited) from the page's own reading ("This page"), and lists its sources. Each milestone has its own link: add `#` and its id, for example `#pacioli` or `#transformer`.

## The rules

- **AQ (Alphanumeric Qabbala):** A = 10 through Z = 35, with digits 0–9 at face value.
- **Other ciphers:** Ordinal A = 1 to Z = 26. Reverse Z = 1 to A = 26. Reduction and Reverse reduction collapse each letter to one digit. Satanic A = 36 to Z = 61.
- **Zones:** a phrase's zone is the digital root of its AQ total (add the digits until one is left). A digital root runs from 1 to 9 and never gives 0, because 9 and 0 are the same number in arithmetic mod 9. The diagram draws them as two zones joined by Uttunul's syzygy, so a phrase on 9 stands for that 9::0 pair.
- **Syzygies:** zones pair up by summing to nine: 9::0 Uttunul, 8::1 Murrumur, 7::2 Oddubb, 6::3 Djynxx, 5::4 Katak.
- **Currents:** each syzygy's difference names the zone its current flows to. Surge (8::1 → 7), Hold (7::2 → 5) and Sink (5::4 → 1) form the Time-Circuit. Warp (6::3 → 3) and Plex (9::0 → 9) fold back on themselves.
- **Gates:** zone n's gate is 0 + 1 + … + n (Gt-00 through Gt-45), and it leads to that number's digital root.
- **Demons:** demon a::b sits at mesh a(a−1)/2 + b, from 00 to 44.

## Some things it shows

- **Man and Ash** score the same in every cipher on a standard gematria calculator. So "What is Man?" and "What is Ash?" are indistinguishable.
- **Xenocosmography, Eloquent Miracles and Dark Enlightenment** all equal 333 in AQ.
- **Every mirror pair of letters sums to 45**, the final gate. So a letter and its mirror always land in syzygy twins, and any Reverse cipher sends a phrase to the twin of its forward zone.
- **"Gt" is itself a mirror pair** (G 16 + T 29 = 45). Written out in AQ, every gate's name lands on the zone that gate opens onto.
- **Every number, spelled out and summed in Ordinal, ends in the same loop:** 216 → 228 → 288 → 255 → 240. On the Numogram that reads 9, 3, 9, 3, 6: it never touches the Time-Circuit and shuttles between the Plex and the Warp.
- **11 of the 45 demons' names land in one of their own zones**, about what chance predicts. But 7 of those 11 fall in the first ten meshes, a run that dense turns up by chance roughly 1 time in 80. That estimate allows for where the run sits but not for choices made after looking, such as the window width; the Demons tab explains the method and shows how the odds move.
- **"Claude Opus," "Alan Turing" and "Divination"** are exact twins: identical in Ordinal, Reverse, Satanic and AQ.

None of this is meant to claim hidden meaning. With hundreds of phrases and only nine zones, coincidences are guaranteed. The fun is in which ones turn up, and the page tries to say how surprising each one is.

## Running it

It's a single self-contained `index.html` with no build step and no dependencies. Open it in any browser, or host it anywhere that serves static files. Fonts load from Google Fonts, and the page falls back to system fonts offline. It works on phones and in light or dark mode.
