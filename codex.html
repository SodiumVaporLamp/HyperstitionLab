AQ 135B3/S23Conway’s Life

Conway’s Life judges every cell by its 3×3 block, which holds 0 to 9 live cells: ten zones, like the Numogram’s. Write what each zone does as one base-3 digit and the rule becomes a number. Conway’s Life is 135, and in AQ, so is CONWAY. Type a word: its AQ total becomes a law, its letters become a body, and the body runs under its own law beside Conway’s.

Its physics

Each column is a syzygy, two zones that sum to nine. Filled: ignite. Outlined: hold. Plain: void. Dots mark the Numogram’s regions: Time-Circuit, Warp, Plex.

    Speed

    Slowed to three generations a second while large areas flicker.

    how a word becomes a universe

    A Life-like rule asks one question of each cell: how many of the nine cells in its 3×3 block, the cell and its eight neighbours, are alive. The answer runs from 0 to 9, the ten digits the Numogram lays out as zones. Conway’s rule, born with 3 neighbours and surviving with 2 or 3, says exactly this in zone terms: zone 3 ignites, zone 4 holds, and every other zone goes void.

    Give each zone a base-3 digit, 0 for void, 1 for hold and 2 for ignite, and read them as one number with zone 0 as the lowest digit. Conway’s Life comes out as 2 × 27 + 1 × 81 = 135. Every number below 59,049 is a rule, and a word’s AQ total picks one. Totals here stay under 6,561, so zones 8 and 9 always void.

    The letters build the starting shape. Each letter or digit becomes a row of live cells as long as its AQ value, from 10 cells for A to 35 for Z, centred and stacked with one empty row between rows; a 0 leaves its row empty. Words with equal totals obey the same law but start from different bodies, which is gematria’s claim made literal: TIME and DEATH are both 83.

    Both dishes run on an unbounded plane. A fate is called when the pattern repeats exactly, as a still life, a cycle or a single traveller, or when its population has repeated for 600 generations while objects fly away. A run that passes 25,000 cells is called an explosion; one still changing after 10,000 generations, or about fifteen seconds of computing, is reported as unsettled.

    what the code can’t do

    Twins

    A live cell always counts itself, so zone 0 only ever judges dead cells, where void and hold mean the same thing. Every multiple of 3 therefore has a twin one above it: 136 is Conway’s Life too, and ORACLE (108) shares GLIDER’s physics (109). Zone 9 is the mirror case. The 59,049 codes give 26,244 different physics.

    No flips

    A zone can void, hold or ignite, but it can’t flip, giving birth to a dead cell while killing a live one at the same count. Rules that need a flip have no number here, among them HighLife (B36/S23), Seeds (B2/S) and Day & Night (B3678/S34678). The code reaches 26,244 of the 262,144 Life-like rules, about one in ten.

    Empty space ignites

    A total that leaves a remainder of 2 when divided by 3 makes zone 0 ignite: every empty cell with no live neighbours is born, so the whole background flips each generation. That happens for 33% of common English words. The dish follows Golly’s method and draws only the cells that differ from the background, so the screen never strobes.

    how lucky is conway?

    Less than it looks. Totals of short words bunch together: the average letter is worth about 20.6 in AQ, so six-letter words average about 124, and 135 is well inside their usual range. Before computing anything, a list of 50 Life terms was fixed (below) to ask how often such a search finds a hit by luck.

    Rules

    AQ
    A = 10 through Z = 35, with digits 0–9 at face value. Accents are dropped before counting, as in Numogram Lab.
    Zones
    A cell’s zone is the number of live cells in its 3×3 block, itself included. A dead cell with n live neighbours sits in zone n; a live one sits in zone n + 1.
    Code
    The AQ total modulo 59,049, read in base 3 with zone 0 as the lowest digit: 0 void (dead next), 1 hold (keep its state), 2 ignite (alive next).
    Body
    One row per letter or digit, as many cells long as its AQ value, starting at x = −⌊L/2⌋, with rows two apart. Other characters are skipped.
    Empty space
    Rules with zone 0 igniting follow Golly’s emulation: without S8 the background flips every generation, with S8 it lights once and stays lit. Counts and drawings show the cells that differ from it.
    Fates
    Exact repeats give still lifes, cycles and travellers. Objects count as thrown off once their surroundings were empty 200 generations earlier and the population has repeated for 600. Runs stop at 25,000 cells or 10,000 generations.
